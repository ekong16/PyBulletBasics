3. The Receipts: Sources for Best Practices
You absolutely should not take my word for it. Here are the foundational papers that established these exact techniques:

Positional Embeddings via Addition:

Source: "Attention Is All You Need" (Vaswani et al., 2017).

The Proof: Section 3.5 explicitly states: "We add the positional encodings to the input embeddings at the bottoms of the encoder and decoder stacks." They proved addition is functionally identical to concatenation, but computationally cheaper.

Global Conditioning (Action Broadcasting) via Addition/Modulation:

Source: "FiLM: Visual Reasoning with a General Conditioning Layer" (Perez et al., 2018). This proved that applying a global condition (like an action or a question) to every spatial feature simultaneously via affine transformations (addition/multiplication) dominates concatenation.

Modern Source: "Scalable Diffusion Models with Transformers" (DiT) (Peebles & Xie, 2022). The state-of-the-art video generation models use AdaLN (Adaptive Layer Norm), which is fundamentally just broadcasting condition vectors (like text prompts or timesteps) and adding them to every image token.
